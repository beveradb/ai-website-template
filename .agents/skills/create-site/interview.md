# The website interview

Ask **one question at a time**, in plain words. Keep it friendly and quick: about 10 minutes. If they say "you decide" or "I don't know", make a sensible choice, say what you chose, and move on. Note the answers. They become `SITE.md`.

1. **What's the website for?** "In a sentence, what do you want the website to do for you?" (e.g. get clients, show your work, tell people about an event)
2. **Who's it for?** "Who will visit it, and what do they want when they arrive?"
3. **The one action.** "What's the main thing you want visitors to do? For example: email you, call you, book, or look at your work."
4. **Name and words.** "What name should be at the top? Do you have a tagline or a short description of what you do?"
5. **Pages.** Suggest a simple set based on the answers so far (often just Home, About, Work/Services and Contact, or even a single page) and ask if that sounds right.
6. **Content they have.** "Do you have text, photos, a logo or reviews you'd like to use? Don't worry if not. We can start with placeholders."
7. **Contact details.** "What contact details should be on the site? Email, phone, social media? Only include what you're happy to make public."
8. **Look and mood.** "Describe how you'd like it to feel in a few words, e.g. calm and natural, bold and colourful, elegant and minimal. Any colours you love or hate?"
9. **Sites they like.** "Any websites you like the look of? Or ones you don't?" Look at these for feel only, never to copy.
10. **Anything else.** "Anything else I should know, or anything you definitely don't want?"

Then summarise their answers in 4–6 lines and ask: "Have I got that right?" Fix anything, then build.
